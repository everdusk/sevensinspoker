About

At this table, a strong hand is no guarantee you’ll survive the round. Texas Hold’em hands, autobattler combat, and a roguelike adventure. Choose your route, create your build, and manage your abilities right in the middle of a poker showdown. The decisions you make during each hand matter just as much as your preparation.

One against four. Face four opponents at once. If even one of them has a stronger hand, you lose the round and take damage. The other enemies take no damage, even if their hands are weaker than yours. Beating three isn’t enough.

Your opponents want you dead, and they don’t have to wait for the showdown. Keep an eye on their anger and be ready to act. Fireball coming your way? Time your counter and send it flying back. But stay sharp: your enemy can return the favor. Every hand becomes a flurry of attacks. Pull off QTE combos, deflect incoming blows, and switch between planning your hand and reacting fast. As long as the cards are on the table, the fight is still on.
